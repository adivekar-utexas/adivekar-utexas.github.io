## Bio

Abhishek Divekar is a {{ jobtitle }} at Amazon in where he has worked since 2017, first as a software engineer and since 2019 in machine learning research. His research on LLM evaluation, synthetic data and vision-language agents has been published at NeurIPS, AAAI and EMNLP, and his scientific contributions have added over USD 550 million to Amazon's revenue. He chairs the IOAI² AI Models Track at the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence and led Team India at IOAI 2025. He holds an M.S. in Computer Science from UT Austin, advised by Greg Durrett.

## Education

- **M.S. in Computer Science (thesis track), The University of Texas at Austin**, 2020–2024. Thesis: *Diverse dataset synthesis using retrieval-augmented language models*, advised by Greg Durrett. Completed while working full-time at Amazon.
- **B.Tech. in Information Technology, VJTI Mumbai**, 2013–2017. Thesis: *Machine learning for anomaly-based network intrusion detection*, advised by Mahesh Shirole.
