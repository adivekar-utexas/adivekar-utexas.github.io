The [International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI)](https://ioai-official.org/) is an annual international science olympiad in which national teams of high-school students compete on AI and machine-learning tasks.

## International Scientific Committee (since December 2025)

I am a member of the IOAI **International Scientific Committee (ISC)**, the body responsible for the scientific content of the olympiad: proposing, reviewing and finalizing competition tasks, and supporting the International Jury in evaluation.

- **Lead, IOAI² AI Models Track (IOAI 2026).** IOAI² is a new track, launched alongside IOAI 2026, in which AI models attempt the olympiad tasks after each student round, ranked separately from the human contestants. As track lead within the ISC, I drafted the competition format and rules and facilitated ISC decisions on the track's scientific design.
- **Task author.** Proposed original competition tasks for IOAI 2026 (for example, *Rosetta 2D*, a 2D masked-sequence-modeling task).

## Team India coach, IOAI 2025

I coached India's national team for IOAI 2025 and contributed to the national selection process, including designing ML strategy tests and conducting interviews in the final selection stages.
