The [International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI)](https://ioai-official.org/) is an annual international science olympiad for high-school students. Its 2026 edition in Astana, Kazakhstan had 108 countries and territories represented.

## IOAI 2026: Chair, IOAI² AI Models Track

At IOAI 2026, IOAI launched **IOAI²: AI Models Track**, the first track at an international science olympiad in which fully autonomous AI systems attempt the same tasks as the student contestants. I served as **Chair of the AI Models Track**.

- **Scale of the inaugural edition:** 15 autonomous AI systems from 14 organizations in 5 countries took part, including Sakana AI, MBZUAI IFM, Shanghai AI Laboratory, Peking University, Westlake University and UCLA.
- **Official statement and report:** I wrote the track's official statement, and I am the lead author (with IOAI cofounder and Board Chair Elena Marinova) of the [IOAI² 2026 Inaugural Edition Review](https://ioai-official.org/ai-models-track/ioai%C2%B2-2026-report/). See also the [AI Models Track page](https://ioai-official.org/ai-models-track/).
- **Rules and materials:** the task adaptations, scoring method, competition rules and Jury precedents are released on [GitHub](https://github.com/IOAI-official/IOAI-AI-Models-Track) for reuse by other olympiads.

Other roles at IOAI 2026, as listed in the [official report](https://ioai-official.org/ai-models-track/ioai%C2%B2-2026-report/):

- **Member, International Scientific Committee (ISC).** The ISC sets the scientific direction of the competition. For the AI Models Track, it approved the rules and oversaw the method used to normalize scores onto a common scale.
- **Member, IOAI Jury.** The Jury verified the AI Models Track submissions.

## IOAI 2025: Team Leader, Team India

I was **Team Leader of Team India** at IOAI 2025 in Beijing, China, and contributed to the final stages of India's national team selection. The eight-member team won **3 Gold, 2 Silver and 1 Bronze medals**, plus one Honourable Mention ([official IOAI 2025 results](https://ioai-official.org/china-2025/results-2025/)).

In the official individual-contest results (284 contestants from 53 countries and regions), India's medals were:

| Rank | Student | Medal |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Roumak Das | Gold |
| 18 | Arjun Tyagi | Gold |
| 19 | Soham Sen | Gold |
| 37 | Rayan Banerjee | Silver |
| 41 | Samik Goyal | Silver |
| 77 | Himaneesh Sompalle | Bronze |

India's 3 golds tied for the second-highest gold count of any country, behind only Russia. Ranked Olympic-style (golds first, then silvers, then bronzes), India placed **3rd of 53 countries and regions**. These figures are computed from the official results table.

A member of the 2025 team, Ishaan Gangwani (Honourable Mention), later co-founded Synthetic Sciences, the lab that won the inaugural IOAI² AI Models Track in 2026 ([report](https://ioai-official.org/ai-models-track/ioai%C2%B2-2026-report/)).
