AI Olympiad

The International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI) is an annual science olympiad for high-school students. Its 2026 edition in Astana, Kazakhstan had 108 countries and territories represented.

  • IOAI 2026 · Astana

    Chair, IOAI² AI Models Track

    The first track at an international science olympiad where fully autonomous AI systems attempt the same tasks as the students. 15 AI systems from 14 organizations in 5 countries took part, including Sakana AI, MBZUAI IFM, Shanghai AI Laboratory, Peking University, Westlake University and UCLA.

    I wrote the track's official statement and am lead author, with IOAI Board Chair Elena Marinova, of the inaugural review. The International Jury's final report at the closing ceremony presented the track and gave special thanks to me and to Kaggle.

    Also: member of the International Scientific Committee and the IOAI Jury

  • IOAI 2025 · Beijing

    Team Leader, Team India

    India's debut: 3 Gold, 2 Silver and 1 Bronze, plus one Honourable Mention, placing 3rd of 53 countries and regions by medal table. I helped select, mentor and coach the eight olympians, ran timed six-hour mock contests, and represented India at the IOAI General Assembly. I also designed the team's peacock logo, one feather per olympian.

    Golds: Roumak Das (rank 11), Arjun Tyagi (18), Soham Sen (19). Silvers: Rayan Banerjee (37), Samik Goyal (41). Bronze: Himaneesh Sompalle (77). Ranks and medal-table position computed from the official results.
